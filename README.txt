# Free Fire Tournament Registration Website

This package gives you:
- A mobile-friendly tournament landing page
- Squad/team registration form
- 4-player names + Free Fire UIDs
- Captain WhatsApp number
- UPI QR/payment section
- Transaction/UTR ID field
- Payment screenshot upload
- Automatic storage of registrations in Google Sheets
- Automatic storage of payment screenshots in Google Drive

## IMPORTANT
The QR image and organizer settings are placeholders. Replace them before publishing.

## STEP 1 — Create your Google Sheet
1. Open Google Sheets.
2. Create a new spreadsheet, e.g. "Free Fire Tournament Registrations".
3. Open Extensions → Apps Script.
4. Delete the existing code and paste the full contents of `Code.gs`.
5. Click Save.
6. Select the `setup` function from the function dropdown and click Run.
7. Allow the Google permissions.
8. Your sheet will get a `Registrations` tab automatically.
9. A Google Drive folder called `Tournament Payment Screenshots` will also be created.

## STEP 2 — Publish the registration API
1. In Apps Script click Deploy → New deployment.
2. Select type: Web app.
3. Execute as: Me.
4. Who has access: Anyone.
5. Click Deploy.
6. Copy the Web app URL.
7. You will paste this URL into `index.html`.

Do not share your Apps Script editor or Google account credentials.

## STEP 3 — Add your QR code
Your provided UPI payment image has been added as `qr.png`. Verify the QR and UPI ID before publishing.

Your QR can be generated by your UPI/payment app. Verify the UPI ID and amount before publishing.

## STEP 4 — Edit tournament details
Open `index.html` and find:

const CONFIG = {
  EVENT_NAME: "YOUR TOURNAMENT NAME",
  ENTRY_FEE: "₹100",
  FORMAT: "Squad / 4 Players",
  MATCH_DATE: "Tomorrow",
  CONTACT: "Your WhatsApp",
  UPI_ID: "YOUR-UPI@upi",
  GOOGLE_APPS_SCRIPT_URL: "PASTE_YOUR_DEPLOYED_APPS_SCRIPT_URL_HERE"
};

Replace these values with your real tournament details.

## STEP 5 — Test before sharing
1. Open the website.
2. Submit one test squad.
3. Check the Google Sheet.
4. Check the Google Drive payment screenshot folder.
5. Check that the screenshot URL opens.
6. Only after this, share the registration link publicly.

## STEP 6 — Put the website online
Easy options:
- GitHub Pages
- Netlify
- Vercel

For GitHub Pages:
1. Create a GitHub account.
2. Create a new public repository.
3. Upload `index.html` and `qr.png`.
4. Open Settings → Pages.
5. Select Deploy from branch → main → root.
6. Save.
7. GitHub will give you a public website URL.

## STEP 7 — Tournament workflow
1. Share the website link on WhatsApp/Instagram/Discord.
2. Players open the link.
3. They scan the QR and pay.
4. They enter the squad/player details.
5. They upload the payment screenshot.
6. Their registration appears in your Google Sheet.
7. You verify payment using the UTR/screenshot.
8. Mark verified teams in your Sheet.
9. Send the room ID/password only to verified teams.

## Security / fairness notes
- Do not ask players for UPI PIN, ATM PIN, OTP, card CVV, passwords, or bank login details.
- Only collect information necessary for the tournament.
- Do not publish player phone numbers or UIDs publicly.
- Verify every payment before confirming a slot.
- Keep a backup of the registration sheet.
